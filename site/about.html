About & compliance

Countercraft is an editorial affiliate shop. We write buyer questions and compare the listings we track against the official Amazon product pages. We do not guess: where a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged.

How we verify

Each listing is resolved from the submitted link to its official Amazon product page. Identity, category, browse node, capacity and price are read from that page. Ratings are shown only where captured from the official listing. Every table row links to its source.

Compliance

  • Disclosure. The Associates statement sits under the masthead on every page and repeats in the footer.
  • Sponsored links. Every outbound product link carries rel="sponsored".
  • Special Links. Outbound links should be tagged Special Links generated in Associates Central; the submitted short links are flagged for review under Program Policies 6(w).
  • Ratings & prices. Ratings require Creators/PA API (6(t)); prices are shown as listed with a date and a live link (2(b)).
  • No tracking cookies. The click counter uses an anonymous local id only.
