Air fryers: three formats, one decision tree

The three air fryers on this site — dual-basket, oven-style, single-basket — compared by format. Every figure below is read from the official listing it links to.

Dual-basket: two dishes, finished together

P1 · Chefman 6 qt dual basket air fryer oven — 2 independent 3 qt nonstick baskets (listed dishwasher-safe), Easy View windows, Sync Finish, Hi-Fry, auto shutoff. Listed dimensions 13.38″D × 11.81″W × 10.63″H.

Choose this format when most meals have two components that want different settings. The trade: each zone is only 3 qt, so nothing large goes in whole.

Oven-style: tray cooking behind French doors

P2 · Emeril Lagasse 26 qt French door air fryer toaster oven — 26 qt listed interior (24.6 L as listed), 24 one-touch preset options (as listed 2026-11-19), six cooking accessories included, stainless steel. Listed dimensions 16.9″D × 15.8″W × 14.8″H — the deepest and tallest machine here.

Choose this format when you cook on trays: vegetables spread out, toast, a small roast. The trade: counter depth, door clearance, and more parts to clean. Its function count has been described more than one way across listing sections (F-26).

Single-basket: one big job, smallest footprint

P3 · Chefman 8 qt TurboFry air fryer — 8 qt listed basket (XL nonstick, dishwasher-safe as listed), 4-in-1 (air fry, bake, dehydrate, frozen), 450°F Hi-Fry option, LED shake reminder. Listed dimensions 12.75″D × 13.5″W × 10″H — the shallowest here.

Choose this format when it's one dish at a time and the counter is spoken for. The trade: no second zone. Note the 6/8/9-quart variant family (F-08) — confirm the 8 qt variant in the cart.
