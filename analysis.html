Analysis and findings

This is the page a reviewer was promised: the register read as findings rather than as rows. Everything on it is computed at build time from the same files the site publishes from, so it cannot drift from the data it summarises — and where the honest answer is "we cannot tell yet", that is the finding.

Five findings, stated plainly

1 · The links are not the problem; the account is

All five submitted short links resolve, on a dated read, to real listings whose titles match this register character for character. What cannot be checked from outside an Associates account is that shopnow047c-20 is registered to this site and that a click through it reports. That single unknown sits under every number this project might one day earn (F-57, F-58).

2 · The published set is complete only for the air fryers

Three of five products carry capacity, dimensions and features read from the listing. The two thermometers carry features and category but no dimensions or capacity — because a thermometer has neither. Blank cells are marked, never filled (method).

3 · Format, not price, is the differentiator here

Prices and ratings are withheld by rule (F-02), which leaves the one comparison buyers genuinely cannot make themselves: what a listed capacity costs in counter inches. That ranking is below, and it is the most useful thing on this site.

4 · Nothing is measured, so nothing is optimised

Five events are defined and counted in the visitor's browser; no endpoint receives them, so the campaign ledger is empty by rule, not by neglect. Every ranking on this page is therefore editorial judgement against the registers — stated as such (Q-P22, F-19).

5 · The site is correct and unconverted

It reads as a careful register: caveats first, product second. The queue's top-ranked items are the ones that put the shelf in front of the reader without touching a single claim (suggestions SG-15, SG-16, SG-19).

What we are waiting on, ranked

These are not open bugs; they are open decisions. Each one is answerable in a sentence and each one moves something below it, which is why they are ordered by what they unblock rather than by how long they have been sitting.

    Decide the register question

    SG-31 / Q-P15 / F-P06 — is data/p2/ the only register that feeds published values?

    Two files disagree about whether prices exist. Until one is canonical, every future pass has to rediscover which one the live page used, and a reviewer cannot tell a stale figure from a deliberate omission.

    Waiting on: A one-line answer in COORDINATION.md. Any session can then move the other file to archive/ and say so on the method page. effort: Minutes

    Then moves: F-P06 SG-31 Q-27

  2. 2

    Decide who reads the suggestions

    Q-P08 — who accepts a path, how often, and does a reviewer's accept bind the build?

    34 items sit in a queue with no named decider. That is the difference between a decision surface and a list nobody is allowed to close, and it is why a prior session's accepted design brief sat unimplemented for two passes.

    Waiting on: A name or a rule. The queue already carries accepted / amended / rejected as states; it just has nobody to set them. effort: One sentence

    Then moves: Every SG item

  3. 3

    Turn on measurement before changing anything else

    SG-06, SG-25 / Q-P06, Q-23 — host the collector and verify a Search Console property.

    Every ranking on this board is currently judgement, because there is no impression or click data. Two weeks of real queries would reorder the content queue on evidence instead of taste.

    Waiting on: A host for one small service and a verified property. The collector already exists in the repository and stores nothing identifying. effort: An afternoon

    Then moves: SG-04 SG-24 SG-11 Q-P02 Q-P07

  4. 4

    Pick the price policy

    Q-27 / SG-05 and F-P06 — dated snapshot prices, a live licensed price, or none at all.

    The number is the most-clicked element on any commercial page. Choosing 'none' is defensible; choosing it by default, because nobody picked, is the current state and it quietly costs the most.

    Waiting on: A decision. If prices appear, they need the date-pairing check and the re-verification cadence agreed first, or the page ages badly. effort: One decision + a gate rule

    Then moves: SG-05 Q-27 the CTA design in DP-2 and DP-3

  5. 5

    Set the re-verification cadence

    Q-P11 — how old may a published figure be before the build refuses it?

    Titles, preset counts and carousels have already been observed drifting between reads days apart. A site whose only claim is 'we checked' has to define what 'checked' is worth in time.

    Waiting on: An interval (a day, a fortnight, a quarter) and who owns the sweep. Then the gate enforces it and the read-date chip on the product block means something. effort: One number

    Then moves: SG-22 SG-20 SG-05

  6. 6

    Name the publisher

    Q-P19 / SG-27 — who is speaking, and does the mandated disclosure use 'I' or an entity name?

    Amazon prescribes the disclosure statement in the first person; the site is run as a project. A reader, a reviewer and the FTC's guidance all expect an identifiable party, and right now there is none.

    Waiting on: The exact string to publish. A name, an entity, or a deliberate 'editorial desk, contact at…'. effort: Minutes

    Then moves: SG-27 SG-34 final form the About page structured-data author

  7. 7

    Decide the imagery question

    Q-P05 / SG-18 — do we license official product images, draw our own diagrams, or stay typographic?

    An affiliate page with no product image looks unfinished, and a shop expects to be shown the thing. Amazon's images are licensed feed content; our own diagrams are ours. The site currently has neither.

    Waiting on: Which route. Then the design prompt DP-1 becomes implementable instead of aspirational. effort: A decision, then a design pass

    Then moves: SG-18 SG-17 DP-1 DP-2

  8. 8

    Confirm the account facts only you can see

    Q-01, F-57, F-58 — enrolment under shopnow047c-20, the site registered to it, one link tested end to end.

    Everything on the site is verifiable except the two things that make it a business: that the tag belongs to this site and that a click through it reports. Both are single sign-ins away from settled.

    Waiting on: A look in Associates Central. A session cannot see it, so the register will carry it as unverified until you do. effort: Ten minutes

    Then moves: F-57 F-58 F-03 to a yes/no every growth item below it

  9. 9

    Close the open pull request hazard

    Q-P18 / SG-32 — PR #16 would overwrite the generated root the live site is served from.

    It is not a content question, it is repository safety: one merge from a stale branch replaces the publication with a hand-authored copy and breaks the reproducibility check that guards it.

    Waiting on: Close it, or ask for it to be retargeted. Logged as RR-5 and RR-6 on the coordination board. effort: Minutes

What this analysis deliberately does not contain

  • No revenue or conversion projection. A figure invented here would be the only unsourced number on the site, and the site is built to have none.
  • No popularity claim. No view counts, no popularity badges, no seasonal assertion without a dated official page behind it (D-19).
  • No ranking of products. The capacity ratio above is a normalisation, not a verdict; the biggest cavity here is also the deepest machine.
  • No prices, no star ratings (F-02).

Read the open questions. Every number above is trustworthy precisely because the missing ones are written down somewhere; the questions page is where the site says what it still does not know, and what it would take to find out.
