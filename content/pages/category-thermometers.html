What accuracy claims don't say

A stated tolerance is a manufacturer claim on a listing — we record it as listed, dated and sourced, and we don't grade it. A NIST certification claim names a calibration chain; the certificate itself isn't on the listing. For what these numbers mean for doneness, read the accuracy guide. For where the probe actually has to go — the part the USDA guidance spends most of its words on — read the long read on placement.
