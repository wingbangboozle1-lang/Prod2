Flagged for review

Open, resolved and standing items with their evidence links. Nothing here blocks publication — this page exists so a human can review the irregularities and unverified values after the site is live and request changes.

How to read a flag

Open = an irregularity or unverified value that stands right now. Resolved = it was closed by a dated action, recorded with evidence. Standing = a rule or a permanent caveat, not a defect. Every row links to the official evidence; the fix-or-accept path is named in the last column.
