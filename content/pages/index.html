What this site is (and isn't)

It's a small, edited buying guide: every figure traces to an official listing with the date it was read, and every product link is a clearly marked paid link. It isn't hands-on testing — nobody here has cooked with these machines (D-06) — and it isn't a deals site: prices and ratings are deliberately unpublished (F-02).
