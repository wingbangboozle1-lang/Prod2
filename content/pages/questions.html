The work behind it

Open questions: what must be answered, and how

Twenty-seven questions sit here. Each one records what is already known (with its dated source), what is missing, what the answer would change, who can give it, and what the site does while nobody has. The ones that gate a decision are listed first.

The short version

Nothing on this list blocks publication: the site is live, correct and conservative, and the blank cells are marked as blank. What these questions gate is everything past "correct" — whether a click can be said to attribute, whether a price may be printed, who is speaking in the disclosure, where measurement data goes, and whether an owner has looked at the nine decisions ranked on the analysis page. If nobody answers, the site keeps publishing the safe version of every one of those, forever.

The full register, with the answer fields: data/p2/questions.json. To answer, fill owner_answer and owner_answer_date on the item — or reply here in the brief or in COORDINATION.md with the question ID and the answer; both are picked up by the next session, and answers become entries in the decision log.

How to read a question that is still open

Open does not mean neglected. It means a session found something it could not verify from an official source, or a decision it had no authority to make, and wrote it down instead of guessing. The corresponding flags carry the evidence; the queue carries what to do about them. That chain — question, flag, suggestion, decision — is the whole method, and it is why the site can afford to leave things blank.
