The counter-space analysis

The spec most listings bury is the one that decides whether you keep the appliance: how much counter it surrenders. Dividing listed capacity by listed footprint gives a like-for-like figure — shown here as a formula you can check, not a score (D-26).

How to read this table

  • Capacity is quoted exactly as the listing states it (quarts, and litres where the listing gives them). Cross-format quart comparisons are slippery — that's what the capacity checklist is for.
  • Dimensions are the listing's D×W×H. Measure your counter, including the clearance some air fryers want behind them.
  • Every row's Source column links to the live official listing; each figure was read on a dated pass (the read dates are on the method page).
  • Blank cells are honest. If a value can't be confirmed from an official source it is left blank and flagged — never inferred.
