How to read the range and accuracy claims

Two figures on this listing deserve a translation before a purchase. "Accurate within 1°F as listed" is a stated tolerance — the accuracy guide compares it, and the other thermometer's ±0.5℉ figure, against what USDA actually emphasises: placement and threshold temperatures, not decimals. And "WiFi unlimited range" describes how the signal travels — through the hub and network, not through any distance you can test in a store. The probe ceiling — 1000°F heat-safe as listed — is quoted here exactly as the listing words it, and nothing else is added. Every figure on this page stays quoted, dated and attributed.
