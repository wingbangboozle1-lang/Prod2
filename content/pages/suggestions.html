Suggestion queue: choose the path before we build

Every proposed improvement, with its evidence, expected impact, effort and risk. This page is the review surface the brief asked for: a place to compare paths and pick one before anything changes.

How to use this queue

Each card ends with our recommendation and the dependencies that gate it. To proceed: pick accept, amend, or reject per item (a comment in the repo's COORDINATION.md, or an instruction in the brief, is enough). Accepted items move to the decision log with their date, then to the build. Items the standing brief has already mandated show accepted or shipped with a progress note instead of waiting for a second nod.

The shortest credible path — the smallest sequence of owner decisions that unblocks the most work (SG-32 makes this standing):

  3. SG-33 — the token layer, immediately after: it makes the pick reversible.
  4. SG-34 — switch topic selection to measured queries after 14 days of data.
  5. SG-36 + SG-04 — open the question-capture loop once the consent basis exists (Q-47).
