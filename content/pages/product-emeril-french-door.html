Who it suits

Trays, racks and company

  • Sheet-pan cooks. Rack cooking spreads food out in a single layer — the shape a basket can't hold. Vegetables, toast, a small roast fit the listed "Extra Large Interior".
  • Holiday and hosting waves. The hosting guide works through how this format handles food arriving faster than the oven can cycle.
  • People who want presets, not dials. 24 one-touch options as listed; the multi-function question list is in the multi-function guide.

Who it doesn't suit

Shallow counters and solo portions

  • Any counter under about 17″ of depth. 16.9″D is listed — 4.15″ deeper than P3 and 3.52″ deeper than P1. Door swing and vent clearance come on top of that.
  • One- or two-person weeknights. A 26 qt cavity and six accessories are more machine, and more washing, than a single 3 qt basket of fries needs; that job is P3's.
  • Two zones on one timer. French doors are one cavity; if two independent finishes are the requirement, that is the dual-basket.
