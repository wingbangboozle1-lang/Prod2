How many quarts do you actually need?

Quarts on a listing describe a cavity, not a crowd. Here's how to turn the listed capacities of these three air fryers into a serving-count sanity check — with the unit trap called out.

The three air fryers on this site state their capacity plainly: P1 lists 6 qt total split into two independent 3 qt baskets; P3 lists one 8 qt basket; P2 lists a 26 qt interior (24.6 liters as listed). Read straight, that's 6, 8 and 26. Read honestly, those numbers describe three different kinds of space.

What a quart claim is actually measuring

  • Basket quarts (P1, P3) count the fillable volume of a drawer. Food can't stack to the rim — air has to move — so usable capacity sits below the number.
  • Oven-cavity quarts (P2) count a box with racks. It fits a sheet pan or a chicken; the 26 qt figure and a 6 qt basket figure were never meant to be compared like for like.
  • Split quarts (P1) are the trap of their own: 6 qt total is two 3 qt zones. You cannot put one 6 qt dish in it.

Unit caution, flagged: P1's listing title states "6 Quart / 2 × 3Qt baskets" (read 2026-11-19) while an earlier spec-table read recorded "6 liters" for the same product — the listing uses both words in different places (F-P04). The figures above follow the title read, and the disagreement is published rather than smoothed over.

A serving-count sanity check

No official source maps quarts to servings, so treat this as arithmetic you do at home with the listed numbers, not a claim from any listing:

  1. List what you actually cook in one sitting: a main and a side, two mains, one big batch of fries.
  2. Split that list against the zones. Two 3 qt zones (P1) = two dishes at once, each sized for one or two people. One 8 qt zone (P3) = one bigger dish, or one batch for four. A 26 qt cavity (P2) = a rack meal that would have taken the full oven.
  3. Check the batch math against your worst weeknight, not your best dinner party. The honest question is how many batches a format saves you, not how many quarts it owns.

What we could not verify

Real-world fill: how much food fits a 3 qt basket in practice. Listings state volume; none of them states servings, and nobody here has cooked with these machines (D-06). The sanity check above is arithmetic, not a test result.

The one-sentence answer

Count zones first and quarts second: two 3 qt drawers, one 8 qt drawer, and a 26 qt rack cavity are three different answers to "what's for dinner," and only one of them is a size question.
