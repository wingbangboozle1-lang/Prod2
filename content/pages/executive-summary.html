The work behind it

Executive summary

What this project is, what is verified, what is missing, and what decisions are open — on one page. Written for a reviewer deciding what to do next.

What this is

A small affiliate publication ({{brand}} — {{descriptor}}) built around five owner-supplied Amazon products: three air fryers (dual-basket, oven-style, single-basket) and two meat thermometers (instant-read, wireless leave-in). The reader-facing site is a question-led buying guide; the machinery behind it — verification register, intent records, flags, open questions, suggestion queue, decision log, measurement plan — stays reachable from one navigation group and every footer.

The five products and their status

  • P1–P3 (ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH) — verified from official listings since 2026-11-14, re-checked on dated passes through 2026-11-20. Specification tables carried from those dated reads; titles and breadcrumbs were re-read three times on 2026-11-20 (records: verification-2026-11-20.json, live-check-2026-11-20-722181aa.json, live-check-2026-11-20-770ebcc2.json) — all three reads match the register verbatim.
  • P4–P5 (ASINs B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z) — the brief's two additional short links, resolved and read for the first time on 2026-11-19. They are meat thermometers, not air fryers; the site organizes by format accordingly (decision D-P01).
  • Links: on 2026-11-19 all five owner-submitted short links resolved to the ASINs above with no tag= parameter (flag F-03). On 2026-11-20 a same-day re-read confirmed one of the five links, and a second same-day read disagrees on two (flags F-P24, F-P27 and F-P28), so the owner should check them in a browser (Q-P15). The site publishes tagged Special Links (https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20, decision D-01) with rel="sponsored" and the required disclosure on every page. Whether the tracking ID is registered and whether clicks attribute is confirmable only inside Associates Central (F-57, F-58) — the site claims no revenue.

What is deliberately absent

  • Prices and star ratings — withheld (F-02): they drift faster than honest re-verification.
  • Commission figures per product — blank (F-01): the official rate table's category bands don't map to these ASINs; no rate is inferred.
  • Hands-on testing — none claimed (D-06): this is a specification publication until real usage exists (queued as SG-07).
  • Forecasts — no conversion score or revenue forecast anywhere; the campaign ledger starts empty by rule.

Measurement and automation

The build is automated end to end: tools/build.py renders the site from the data registers; tools/checks.py is the publish gate (disclosure placement, sponsored-link marking, unique titles/meta, sourced facts, link integrity, markup validity and register cross-references) and runs from a clean checkout with standard-library Python. Measurement ships as machinery but not as data: the privacy-preserving click counter and the self-hosted endpoint are deployable (contract here), and nothing is transmitted until an endpoint is configured (F-19). The five defined ledger events and their official attribution windows are recorded on the measurement page.

The 2026-11-19 publication pass

The reader-facing site now leads with the products: a shop-first front page (product cards before any data), a guides hub with two content clusters and a seasonal desk, ten guide pages on a 14-day cadence, cleaner navigation, RSS subscribe affordances, and a consent-gated question form that sends nothing until its privacy basis exists (D-P07). Nothing moved on the machinery: disclosure above the fold everywhere, tagged rel="sponsored" Special Links only, prices and ratings still withheld, and every published figure still carries its source and read date.

The 2026-11-20 reader pass (this session)

Three changes, all inside the same discipline. (1) Every product now has its own page — listed-spec table with read dates, who it suits and who it does not, the counter or claim math, and its own "what we could not verify" block (SG-12 completed beyond its card-level shipping note; content policy recorded as D-P24: register values only, no offers, no ratings, no performance language). The shelf cards, both category pillars, the comparison table and the footer all route into them, and the new productpages gate section keeps one page per product in lockstep. (2) Third same-day live re-read: all five /dp/ titles and breadcrumbs match the register character-for-character; all five owner-submitted short links again failed to resolve through the sandbox's only reader, so F-P24 stays open and the buy path stays the tagged /dp/ URLs. (3) The measurement contract, the ranked decision board and the two long reads from earlier passes are unchanged.

Still owner-only: Settings → Pages → Source: GitHub Actions (F-P19/F-P20), the one-link Associates Central test (F-58, and it is also the single check that settles whether a real product photo can be shown — Q-P16/SG-47), hosting the endpoint (F-19), and Search Console (F-P13, SG-44).

The 2026-11-20 editorial and governance pass

Three reader pages added, every one sourced from official guidance the listings do not carry: a doneness reference built on the USDA FSIS temperature chart (what temperature counts as done), a leftovers guide built on the FSIS leftovers factsheet (how long is safe), and a long read on the two-hour rule (the Danger Zone) — the third in that series, so a reader has three arguments rather than a shelf of checklists. The front page, the hub and the buying guide now render long reads and the hero counts from the registers instead of typing them, because typed numbers were the first thing to go stale.

Machinery: FAQPage markup is now derived from each page's own visible Q&A block and gated against it, which also closed a live defect where the landing node answered 4 of 6 visible questions and the decision page described 4 questions no reader could see (F-P36). Placeholder values left the product structured data (F-P34). Paragraph balance became a gate rule after it found the same orphaned-sentence defect on the shop page that the hub had shipped (F-P30). And the editorial calendar is enforced: a publish that does not advance the schedule, or a queue slot that passes its date unreconciled, fails the build (F-P34, D-P28).

Review surface: the suggestion page gained a generated decision-readiness table — every live proposal against the question that blocks it, who can answer, and the cheapest route to the answer — and it states the queue's real counts instead of a typed number. Four open questions (Q-P40 manuals, Q-P41 cadence resourcing, Q-P42 FAQ framing, Q-P43 published-branch contents) and seven proposals (SG-52…SG-58) carry what this pass could not settle. Nothing was rounded up, and nothing that could not be read was written.
