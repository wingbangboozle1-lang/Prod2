The work behind it

Executive summary

What this project is, what is verified, what is missing, and what decisions are open — on one page. Written for a reviewer deciding what to do next.

What this is

A small affiliate publication ({{brand}} — {{descriptor}}) built around five owner-supplied Amazon products: three air fryers (dual-basket, oven-style, single-basket) and two meat thermometers (instant-read, wireless leave-in). The reader-facing site is a question-led buying guide; the machinery behind it — verification register, intent records, flags, open questions, suggestion queue, decision log, measurement plan — stays reachable from one navigation group and every footer.

The five products and their status

  • P1–P3 (ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH) — verified from official listings since 2026-11-14, re-checked on dated passes, most recently live on 2026-11-20. Specification tables carried from those dated reads; titles and breadcrumbs re-read 2026-11-19.
  • P4–P5 (ASINs B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z) — the brief's two additional short links, resolved and read for the first time on 2026-11-19 and re-read live on 2026-11-20. They are meat thermometers, not air fryers; the site organizes by format accordingly (decision D-P01).
  • Links: all five owner-submitted short links resolve to the ASINs above with no tag= parameter on either the 2026-11-19 or the 2026-11-20 read (flags F-03, F-P16). The site publishes tagged Special Links (https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20, decision D-01) with rel="sponsored" and the required disclosure on every page. Whether the tracking ID is registered and whether clicks attribute is confirmable only inside Associates Central (F-57, F-58) — the site claims no revenue.

What is deliberately absent

  • Prices and star ratings — withheld (F-02), and now withheld for a checked reason rather than a policy of caution: Program Policies 2(b) and Participation Requirements 6(t) were re-read on 2026-11-20 and rule out a value observed on a page and re-rendered by us (D-P10, the rule in full).
  • Product photography — never copied from a listing (Amazon's IP License) and never staged: every image is the site's own artwork (SG-40).
  • Commission figures per product — blank (F-01): the official rate table's category bands don't map to these ASINs; no rate is inferred.
  • Hands-on testing — none claimed (D-06): this is a specification publication until real usage exists (queued as SG-07).
  • Forecasts — no conversion score or revenue forecast anywhere; the campaign ledger starts empty by rule.

Measurement and automation

The build is automated end to end: tools/build.py renders the site from the data registers; tools/checks.py is the publish gate (disclosure placement, sponsored-link marking, unique titles/meta, sourced facts, link integrity, markup validity and register cross-references) and runs from a clean checkout with standard-library Python. Measurement ships as machinery but not as data: the privacy-preserving click counter and the self-hosted endpoint are deployable (contract here), and nothing is transmitted until an endpoint is configured (F-19). The five defined ledger events and their official attribution windows are recorded on the measurement page.

Open decisions for the reviewer

Ordered by what unblocks most. Each is one decision; the suggestion table gives the cost, the reversibility and the exact question for every item.

  1. Run the one-link-per-product test in Associates Central — gates attribution claims and scaling (Q-01, F-58).
  2. Host the analytics endpoint + enable Search Console — top operational priority; the Search Console checklist is ready on the measurement page (SG-06, Q-P08).
  3. Pick the design direction and the brand name together — three specified directions in SG-31; brand rename after clearance (SG-01, blocked on Q-19).
  4. Settle the reader-capture consent basis — the form ships dark until then (Q-47, F-P14).
  5. Prices, ratings and product photography — one decision, not three — all three become lawful through the same route (Creators/PA API). SG-38, SG-39, SG-40, Q-P10.
  6. Will this ever be a tested publication? — the largest honest upgrade available, and the one claim a competitor cannot copy from a listing. SG-41.
  7. Visual pass: implement or amend the design brief — SG-15 with SG-16, after the brief's cross-references are reconciled (F-P12).

The 2026-11-20 pass (this one)

All five links and listings were re-read live; every ASIN, title and breadcrumb matched, so no published value changed. The two open review requests on prices and ratings were closed against the official policy text. Two real defects were found and fixed: three of the five measurement events were being silently rejected by the collector that would have received them (F-P21), and the preserved reference build was claiming this site's canonical URLs, so a crawler could have indexed a duplicate (F-P20). The front page was rebuilt as a publication front with an explicit five-item shelf, and every image on the site is now the site's own artwork.

The 2026-11-19 publication pass

The reader-facing site now leads with the products: a shop-first front page (product cards before any data), a guides hub with two content clusters and a seasonal desk, eight question-shaped guides on a 14-day cadence, cleaner navigation, RSS subscribe affordances, and a consent-gated question form that sends nothing until its privacy basis exists (D-P07). Nothing moved on the machinery: disclosure above the fold everywhere, tagged rel="sponsored" Special Links only, prices and ratings still withheld, and every published figure still carries its source and read date.
