The work behind it

Executive summary

What this project is, what is verified, what is missing, and what decisions are open — on one page. Written for a reviewer deciding what to do next.

What this is

A small affiliate publication ({{brand}} — {{descriptor}}) built around five owner-supplied Amazon products: three air fryers (dual-basket, oven-style, single-basket) and two meat thermometers (instant-read, wireless leave-in). The reader-facing site is a question-led buying guide; the machinery behind it — verification register, intent records, flags, open questions, suggestion queue, decision log, measurement plan — stays reachable from one navigation group and every footer.

The five products and their status

  • P1–P3 (ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH) — verified from official listings since 2026-11-14, re-checked on dated passes through 2026-11-20. Specification tables carried from the 2026-11-14 read; titles, ASINs and breadcrumbs re-read on every dated pass, most recently 2026-11-20.
  • P4–P5 (ASINs B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z) — the brief's two additional short links, resolved and read for the first time on 2026-11-19. They are meat thermometers, not air fryers; the site organizes by format accordingly (decision D-P01).
  • Links: on 2026-11-19 all five owner-submitted short links resolved to the ASINs above with no tag= parameter (flag F-03). On 2026-11-20 a same-day re-read confirmed one of the five links, and a second same-day read disagrees on two (flags F-P24, F-P27 and F-P28), so the owner should check them in a browser (Q-P18). The site publishes tagged Special Links (https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20, decision D-01) with rel="sponsored" and the required disclosure on every page. Whether the tracking ID is registered and whether clicks attribute is confirmable only inside Associates Central (F-57, F-58) — the site claims no revenue.

What is deliberately absent

  • Prices and star ratings — withheld (F-02): they drift faster than honest re-verification.
  • Commission figures per product — blank (F-01): the official rate table's category bands don't map to these ASINs; no rate is inferred.
  • Hands-on testing — none claimed (D-06): this is a specification publication until real usage exists (queued as SG-07).
  • Forecasts — no conversion score or revenue forecast anywhere; the campaign ledger starts empty by rule.

Measurement and automation

The build is automated end to end: tools/build.py renders the site from the data registers; tools/checks.py is the publish gate (disclosure placement, sponsored-link marking, unique titles/meta, sourced facts, link integrity, markup validity and register cross-references) and runs from a clean checkout with standard-library Python. Measurement ships as machinery but not as data: the privacy-preserving click counter and the self-hosted endpoint are deployable (contract here), and nothing is transmitted until an endpoint is configured (F-19). The five defined ledger events and their official attribution windows are recorded on the measurement page.

The 2026-11-20 evidence pass

Two things were fixed that a reader never sees and a reviewer should not have to take on trust.

  • The live host was serving the project's own working files. Pages publishes this repository's root, so data/p2/settings.json, the coordination board and every archived build were fetchable by URL — measured, not assumed. The setting that controls it cannot be changed with the access these sessions have (a PUT to the Pages API returns 403), so the fix is in the repository: a generated root _config.yml whose exclusion list is derived from the tree, so the build serves only the publication (decision D-P24). It also stops serving a second copy of every page under /docs/. What remains is owner-side tidiness (F-P29).
  • Product copy is now checked against its evidence. A new audit matches every card bullet against the verbatim strings recorded for that ASIN: 11 of 15 are in the listing title a reader can open today, 4 come from a dated read of the spec table, and 0 are unsupported. Six phrases with no recorded evidence — "magnetic back", "rinse-clean", "Smart Hub", "rack cooking", "in the basket lids", "leave-in wireless probes" — were rewritten across the shelf, the comparison table and three guides, and the gate refuses them if they return. The audit and the rule are published: data/p2/claim-audit-2026-11-20.json, F-P30, Q-P19.
  • A third long read — the two-hour rule for leftovers — written from the USDA FSIS factsheet read the same day, including the sentence that names a saucepan, a microwave and an oven, and does not name an air fryer.
  • The publication's voice rules are gated like its facts: a filler blacklist, an em-dash budget, and a requirement that a page opens on the reader's problem rather than on our verification process. The gate runs 15 sections.

The 2026-11-19 publication pass

The reader-facing site now leads with the products: a shop-first front page (product cards before any data), a guides hub with two content clusters and a seasonal desk, ten guide pages on a 14-day cadence, cleaner navigation, RSS subscribe affordances, and a consent-gated question form that sends nothing until its privacy basis exists (D-P07). Nothing moved on the machinery: disclosure above the fold everywhere, tagged rel="sponsored" Special Links only, prices and ratings still withheld, and every published figure still carries its source and read date.
