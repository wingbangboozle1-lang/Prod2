The work behind it

Design prompt pack

Eight copy-ready briefs for the next design pass, and the constraints that survive any redesign. They exist because the site has a real visual identity already — warm paper, serif display, a terracotta accent — and the gap is not taste. It is a shop shelf, honest evidence presentation, measured iteration, and prose that does not sound machine-made. Hand one block to a designer or an agent and you get a result you can check.

Where this came from

Nothing here is invented for the occasion. The prompts are assembled from decisions already recorded in this repository (the direction chosen in D-18, the voice standard in D-12, the evidence rule in D-19) and from the compliance duties the gate already enforces. The matching proposals sit in the suggestion queue — each block names the items it depends on, so a redesign that has not been accepted stays a proposal.

Two things this pack deliberately does not promise

It does not promise traffic. There is no analytics data for this site, so no prompt claims a click-through or ranking improvement; improvement is argued from guidance and from what the page does for a reader (Q-P34). It does not use product photography. Amazon's listing images are Program Content, and generated pictures of a product would be a fabrication — the imagery question stays open as Q-P05, so the identity has to carry the page.
