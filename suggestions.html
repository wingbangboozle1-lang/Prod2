Decision sequence recommended

  1. Verify product intent and Special Links. Identify each intended product independently, confirm official listing and ASIN, then test one enrolled Associates Special Link per product and verify its tracking report. Keep intent and redirect records distinct. No links go into shopper CTAs until this gate is met.
  2. Establish measurement before optimizing. Deploy an approved, privacy-conscious first-party analytics endpoint and Search Console. Agree on data retention and access. Check that events are measured correctly before using them to choose topics.
  3. Publish useful editorial steadily. Proposed experiment: publish one question-led guide each week for four weeks, with clear citations and no unsupported product claims. Add every approved article to the sitemap and RSS feed; measure query impressions and useful engagement after indexing.
  4. Review evidence, then decide. After a defined reporting window, compare first-party search queries and sessions with reader behavior. Use a dated provenance note; expand only topics showing relevant demand and reader utility. No conversion score or revenue forecast is proposed.

Questions that must be answered before a reasonable product decision

What exact products did the five supplied links intend?

Known: only five URLs as submitted. Unknown: ASIN, title, model, destination, listing status, variants, and whether the same product is represented more than once. Discover: confirm each in the enrolled workflow, capture official listing URL and date, and separately record observed destination.

Which buyer are these guides serving?

Known: project examples concern air-fryer and multi-function appliance questions. Unknown: intended audience, household needs, price range, geography, and actual search questions. Discover: review Search Console query data after verification; interview a small set of target readers; do not substitute invented personas for evidence.

What is the real performance baseline?

Known: no campaign measurements are present. Unknown: eligible sessions, outbound clicks, orders, search impressions, and revenue. Discover: configure approved analytics and Search Console, import only authorized aggregate reports, and retain a dated source/provenance record.

What evidence can support comparisons?

Known: nothing product-specific has been independently verified here. Unknown: official dimensions, use/care instructions, included accessories, current pricing, and reader-relevant differences. Discover: use exact-model manufacturer manuals and official listings; record source and retrieval date per fact; leave unconfirmed facts blank.

What operational and compliance boundaries apply?

Known: Amazon and FTC guidance are relevant and linked below. Unknown: account enrollment, API eligibility, chosen analytics host, privacy obligations, review cadence, and business goals. Discover: consult current agreements and policies and obtain qualified privacy/legal advice where needed; do not infer permission from technical capability.
