The work behind it

Suggestions: the queue, and how to choose

Thirty-four proposals, each with two or three concrete paths. This page is where a redesign decision gets made: pick a path per item, and the build picks it up. Nothing here is implemented until a reviewer names a path — that rule is why the items below are still proposals rather than fait accompli.

How to use this queue

Each item carries a problem, a proposal, its evidence, the impact and effort as judged against what the registers actually contain, and then the paths: what changes, the upside, the downside, the cost, and what it unlocks. The recommended path is marked; it is a recommendation, not a decision made for you.

To accept, reply with the item and the path — SG-15, path A is enough, in the brief or in COORDINATION.md. A named reviewer can also set owner_decision and owner_decision_date on the item in data/p2/suggestions.json, and that shows up on the card and on the board below. To amend, say what changes; to reject, say so and the item moves to the decision log with a reason. The unanswered questions that gate several of these are on the open questions page.
