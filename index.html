Who writes this

The Crisp Counter is an edited buying guide, not a store and not a lab. The editing rule is short: every figure on this site was read from an official product listing or an official policy page on a dated pass, and the citation sits next to it. Nobody here has cooked with these machines (D-06), so nothing pretends otherwise. The voice standard is published in the repo (VOICE.md), and the receipts stay visible on the method page.
