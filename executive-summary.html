The work behind it

Executive summary

What this project is, what is verified, what is missing, and what decisions are open — on one page. Written for a reviewer deciding what to do next.

What this is

A small affiliate publication (Prod2 — Kitchen notes & buying guides) built around five owner-supplied Amazon products: three air fryers (dual-basket, oven-style, single-basket) and two meat thermometers (instant-read, wireless leave-in). The reader-facing site is a shop-shaped set of buying guides — a product rail on the front page, eight question-led guides, format pages, a comparison table — and the machinery behind it (verification register, intent records, flags, open questions, the suggestion queue, the decision log, the measurement plan) stays reachable from one navigation group and every footer. The analysis page is where the register is read back as findings rather than as rows.

Verified status, as of the last pass

  • P1–P3 (ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH) — verified from official listings since 2026-11-14, re-checked on dated passes through 2026-11-19. Every published figure carries the date it was read.
  • P4–P5 (ASINs B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z) — the brief's two additional short links, resolved and read for the first time on 2026-11-19. They are meat thermometers, not air fryers; the site organizes by format accordingly (decision D-P01).
  • Links: all five owner-submitted short links resolve to the ASINs above with no tag= parameter (flag F-03). The site publishes tagged Special Links (https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20, decision D-01) with rel="sponsored" and the required disclosure on every page. Whether the tracking ID is registered and whether clicks attribute is confirmable only inside Associates Central (F-57, F-58) — the site claims no revenue. The five re-resolutions are logged with dates in data/p2/verification-log.json.

What is deliberately absent

  • Prices and star ratings — withheld (F-02): they drift faster than honest re-verification. The buttons say "see it on Amazon" instead of quoting a number.
  • Commission figures per product — blank (F-01): the official rate table's category bands don't map to these ASINs; no rate is inferred.
  • Hands-on testing — none claimed (D-06): this is a specification publication until real usage exists (queued as SG-07).
  • Product photography — none hosted: Amazon-supplied assets only, no scraping, no generated food imagery that would imply a kitchen nobody cooked in (queued as SG-08).
  • Forecasts — no conversion score or revenue forecast anywhere; the campaign ledger starts empty by rule and only takes values from an authorized first-party report.

What the last pass changed

  • The decision-support layer became a review surface. Every suggestion now carries two or three priced paths (change, upside, downside, cost, what it unlocks) and a marked recommendation; every question records what is known, what is missing, what it decides, who can answer and the default while it stays open. Accept one by replying SG-15, path A — nothing that changes a claim is adopted before you do (D-P07).
  • The front page leads with products. Product rail, format tiles, guide shelf, then the sourced table — the verification machine moved to where a reader can check it, not where they land (D-P08).
  • Three new guides and a guide index (dual vs single basket, probe in an air fryer, the gift checklist), each with per-figure read dates and a "what we did not test" note.
  • The design prompt pack now publishes the briefs the suggestion queue feeds, with the guardrails a restyle may not remove.
  • Two new gates in tools/checks.py: a dated-figure check on the register tables, and in-page anchor resolution, so a link that scrolls nowhere fails the build.

Measurement and automation

The build is automated end to end: tools/build.py renders the site from the data registers; tools/checks.py is the publish gate (disclosure placement, sponsored-link marking, unique titles/meta, sourced facts, link integrity) and runs from a clean checkout with standard-library Python. Measurement ships as machinery but not as data: the privacy-preserving click counter and the self-hosted endpoint are deployable (contract here), and nothing is transmitted until an endpoint is configured (F-19). The five defined ledger events and their official attribution windows are on that page.

Open decisions for the reviewer

Ranked, with what each is waiting on and what moves once it is answered: the priority board. In short:

  1. Which register is canonical — the one question that decides whether any figure on a future page can be trusted to have come from the file the build used (F-P06).
  2. Who accepts a suggestion — the queue has states and no named decider (Q-P08).
  3. Host the collector and verify a Search Console property — until then every ranking here is judgement, not measurement (SG-06).
  4. Pick the price policy — dated snapshots, a licensed live price, or none (SG-05).
  5. Set the re-verification cadence — the listings have been observed drifting within days (Q-P11).
  6. Name the publisher — the mandated statement is in the first person and the site has no "I" yet (Q-P19).
  7. Close the pull-request hazard — PR #16 would overwrite the generated root the live site is served from (Q-P18); logged as RR-5/RR-6 on the coordination board.
