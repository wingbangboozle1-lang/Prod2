Capacity checklist · 5 minute read

A no-hype checklist for comparing air-fryer capacity

Compare published capacity carefully: check dimensions, usable cooking area, batch guidance, and your real meal size instead of relying on a headline number.

This is a decision framework, not a product test or recommendation. Verify current, model-specific facts in official manufacturer materials and the retailer listing. We have resolved the five submitted short links to Amazon listings, but have not confirmed which products were intended (FR-02).

Work through the evidence

What does “capacity” mean on this listing?

Record the exact wording, unit, and model variant. Do not convert unlike measurements into a direct comparison unless the source provides a sound method.

What are the interior dimensions?

Look for manufacturer-published dimensions for the cooking area or basket, not just the outside footprint. If they are not published, leave the comparison cell blank.

How is food arranged?

Check the manual for load arrangement and any layering or batch guidance. Do not assume a larger stated volume means more usable surface area.

What meal am I sizing for?

Use a familiar meal and the portion size you actually prepare. Treat your needs as a personal constraint, not a product claim.

Can I compare like with like?

Confirm model number, included accessories, and measurement units. Variants can differ; a similar name is not proof of identical specifications.

Keep the comparison honest

Capacity is one input, not a verdict. Pair it with footprint, clearance, cleaning, and the number of batches you are willing to make.
