The desk

About The Counter Edit

A small editorial desk covering the air-fryer aisle properly: question-shaped guides, sourced facts, and a public ledger of what we don’t know.

Why this site exists

The air-fryer aisle is loud. Every listing shouts a quart number, a preset count and a badge. We wanted the opposite: a quiet page that answers the question you actually have, links every claim to its official source, and admits blank cells instead of inventing numbers.

Our voice

  • Plain-spoken. No hype, no urgency theater, no robot prose. If a section reads like a human wrote it for a friend, it passed.
  • Source-first. Product facts come from official Amazon listings, verified on a dated fetch. Every table row on the data pages carries its source link.
  • Opinions labeled. “Best for” is editorial judgment and says so.
  • Blank beats invented. Unverifiable values stay empty and are flagged publicly on the Flagged for Review page.

Who writes this

The Counter Edit desk. We don’t put a personal name on these pages yet — and we won’t invent one. When a real human takes ownership of the byline, their name goes here with a bio that says how they know appliances. Until then, the editorial standards above are the byline.

How the site makes money

Outbound product links to Amazon.com are affiliate links carrying tracking ID shopnow047c-20. When a reader clicks one and buys something, Amazon may pay us a commission. The price you pay is never changed by this. Full details on the disclosure page.

What we will never do

  • Publish a price, rating or dimension we cannot verify from an official source.
  • Repeat a “trending” or “best seller” claim without a dated link to the page that showed it.
  • Use product photography we did not take or license — illustrations only until the photography decision (D-22) is made.
  • Publish a conversion score or revenue forecast — the metrics plan forbids it.
