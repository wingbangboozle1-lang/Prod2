Executive summary

What this is. An Amazon Associates publication (tracking ID shopnow047c-20) built around five products whose submitted short links were resolved live to official listings on 2026-11-19: three air fryers (CHEFMAN 6 Qt dual basket, Emeril Lagasse 26 QT french-door oven, Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry) and two meat thermometers (ThermoMaven instant-read, CHEF iQ Sense wireless). The site is a publication that reads like a shop: four question-shaped guides, a three-question chooser, a five-product comparison, and a product shelf — with the entire verification apparatus reachable from the footer instead of in the reader’s face.

What is verified. Every product fact shown on reader pages was read from the product’s official Amazon listing (title, category path) on 2026-11-19. The submitted ASIN strings do not resolve as direct amazon.com/dp/ paths (404 observed); the link.amazon short links do resolve, and their observed destinations are recorded in a separate field — never merged with the intended ASIN. The compliance status of the short links themselves is unresolved (F-02); reader-facing links therefore use tagged amazon.com/dp/ Special Links built from the observed ASINs.

What is deliberately blank. Prices, star ratings, dimensions and warranty terms. Prices and ratings change daily (flag F-05); dimensions and warranty have not been verified from official sources (F-04, F-06). The capacity-per-centimetre analysis (S-23) publishes its methodology now and its numbers when the inputs verify.

What is measured. Five privacy-preserving events (page views, outbound product clicks, chooser completions, half-reads, disclosure views) queue locally in the browser; a self-hosted aggregate endpoint can be attached later via one config value. No conversion scores, rankings or revenue forecasts exist anywhere in this project (decision D-07) — conversion rows enter the campaign ledger only from authorized first-party reports with a provenance note.

What needs a human. Two owner actions unlock the next stage: verifying the site in Google Search Console (T-10) so topic selection switches from instinct to measured queries, and testing one Special Link per product in the Associates console (T-09) to confirm destination and tracking reporting before scale. Two decisions are reserved for the owner: the brand name (T-11) and the photography policy (T-12) — both are staged on the suggestions page with the evidence for each path.
