Executive summary

Issue 1 · 19 November 2026

Countertop Dispatch is a five-product affiliate publication for kitchen appliances: three air fryers and two meat thermometers, each checked against its official Amazon listing on 19 November 2026. This page is the sixty-second version; everything it claims is one click away from its source.

What we did

  • Resolved all five submitted links live. Each short link landed on a real, in-stock listing; destinations recorded in submitted-links.json.
  • Read each official listing for title, brand, category node, capacity, dimensions and stated features — sources S-14…S-18.
  • Tested every Special Link with the tracking tag shopnow047c-20: five of five resolved correctly.
  • Fetched the governing policies — Operating Agreement, Program Policies, the commission schedule, Google's outbound-link and snippet guidance, and the FTC disclosure guides.
  • Built the publication: front page, product table, two category dashboards, comparison chooser, four question-shaped guides, analysis, suggestions and this summary — all linked, all sourced.

What a reader gets

  • Product rows where every cell either carries an official source link or is visibly blank and flagged.
  • Four guides that answer real buying questions without restating prices or ratings.
  • A three-question chooser that names the listing row its pick rests on.
  • A public flag list — eight irregularities, each with evidence and an owner action.
