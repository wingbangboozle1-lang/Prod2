What we found

Three things stand out from this issue’s checks, all from official pages:

  • The submitted short links resolve, but their tokens are 9 characters — official Amazon documentation defines an ASIN as 10 characters. We recorded what each link resolved to and flagged the mismatch rather than papering over it. FLG-P01
  • The set is two categories, not one: three air fryers and two meat thermometers, each under its official browse node. See the thermometer view
  • Prices, ratings and per-product commission rates are blank on purpose. Amazon’s Program Policies restrict how those may be shown, so they live behind the listing link instead. Read the compliance note
