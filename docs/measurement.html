Measurement: events, click counter and campaign ledger

What gets counted, what never gets counted, and why the campaign ledger is empty on purpose. No conversion score and no revenue forecast is published anywhere on this site.

Two event sets, never mixed

1. The campaign ledger's five defined events — the attribution vocabulary, with the official windows from the Commission Income Statement (S-COMM):

EventDefinitionAttribution window (official)
impressionA tracked unit was shown to a visitor (client-side, first-party only). Recorded by the local tracker; carries no attribution on its own.n/a
clickA visitor activated a Special Link on this site. Starts the attribution session.Session opens at the click and ends at the first of: 24 hours elapsing, an order being placed, or the customer clicking another publisher's Special Link.
sessionThe attribution session record opened by a click, as Amazon measures it.24 hours / order / competing Special Link, whichever comes first (Commission Income Statement 1(b)).
orderA Qualifying Purchase attributed to a Special Link: 1-Click purchases during the session, or cart orders completed no later than 89 days after the initial click.During the session, or up to 89 days for cart orders; the product must ship and be paid for within 180 days of purchase (Commission Income Statement 1(b)(iii)).
refundA returned or refunded order, which reduces reported commission; recorded only when the Associates report states it.As reported by Associates Central.

Windows as stated in the Commission Income Statement (S-COMM). This ledger starts empty and is filled only from authorized first-party reports.

2. The site's five local events — what the click counter can observe first-hand (counts only, no visitor data):

EventDefinitionFields
page_viewA page finished loading.path
outbound_clickA visitor clicked a sponsored product link.path, asin, target_host
guide_read_75A reader scrolled through 75% of a guide.path
category_clickA reader clicked a category tile.path, target
feed_clickA reader clicked the RSS/feed link.path

The privacy-preserving outbound-click counter

assets/js/measure.js counts the five local events with these rules enforced in code:

  • no cookies, no visitor identifiers, no referrer capture;
  • an outbound click records only the ASIN and the target host — never the full URL;
  • nothing leaves the browser while no analytics endpoint is configured — events are counted in the visitor's own localStorage only.

Status: machinery shipped, endpoint not yet hosted (F-19). While data/p2/settings.json → analytics_endpoint is empty, the site sends nothing anywhere.

The self-hosted analytics endpoint

analytics-endpoint/server.py (standard-library Python) accepts exactly the five local events, validates the body (event name, site path, ASIN format), and stores daily counts per event and path. It never reads or stores the client address, user agent, referrer or cookies, and it writes no request log. Deploy it on any owner-controlled HTTPS host, set the endpoint URL in data/p2/settings.json, rebuild — done.

Publishing cadence and what gets written next

  • Cadence: at least one question-shaped guide per month, each added to the sitemap and the feed at publish (both are generated by the build, so a new guide can't ship without them).
  • Topic selection: once measurement is live (SG-06), the next topic is chosen from measured first-party queries — Search Console plus the endpoint's page counts — not from instinct. Until then the queue holds editorial hypotheses labeled as hypotheses (Q-P02).
  • What we never publish: conversion scores, revenue forecasts, or any estimated earnings. Per the Operating Agreement, no representation is made about expected traffic or commission income.

What the owner needs to do to turn this on

  1. Confirm tracking ID shopnow047c-20 in Associates Central; run the one-link-per-product test (protocol: generate/validate one Special Link per product, confirm destination, confirm a test click in reporting) — F-58.
  2. Host analytics-endpoint/server.py; set the endpoint; rebuild — SG-06.
  3. Verify the site in Google Search Console; submit sitemap.xml.
  4. Export the first authorized report into the ledger (timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks, reported orders, report reference, obtained by).
