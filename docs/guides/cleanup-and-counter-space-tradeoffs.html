How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

By the Editorial team (no named author supplied, FR-11)

Cleanup and counter space decide whether an appliance gets used. This guide lists what to check, and what the listings say.

Cleanup, as the listings state it

  • Chefman 6-quart dual-basket air fryer: “Dishwasher Safe Frying Baskets” in the title.
  • Chefman 8-quart TurboFry air fryer: “XL Nonstick Dishwasher-Safe Basket” in the title.
  • Emeril Lagasse 26QT French door air fryer toaster oven: “Six Cooking Accessories Included” in the title. The title does not say the accessories are dishwasher-safe, so check the listing before you assume it.

Counter space

We did not extract listing dimensions (FR-08), so no size comparison is made here. Measure your counter depth and the clearance the appliance needs, then compare with the dimensions section of each listing.

Trade-offs to write down

  1. How often will you use it? Daily use changes how much cleaning effort matters.
  2. Where will it live? A permanent spot and a cupboard-stored appliance need different footprints.
  3. Will accessories fit in your dishwasher, and will they fit in a cupboard once stored?
  4. Does the manufacturer’s manual ask for clear space around the appliance? Check the manual, not only the listing.
