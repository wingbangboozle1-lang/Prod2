Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

By the Editorial team (no named author supplied, FR-11)

Multi-function appliances list many functions. The useful question is which of them you will use. This guide shows the claims printed on the listings so you can check them.

Claims printed on the listings

  • Emeril Lagasse 26QT French door air fryer toaster oven: “24 One-Touch Preset Options” and “Six Cooking Accessories Included”.
  • Chefman 8-quart TurboFry air fryer: “Bake, Dehydrate, Frozen” and a “450°F Hi-Fry Option”.

Questions to ask

  1. Which functions will you use each week? Write them down before you look at preset counts.
  2. Does the maximum temperature on the listing cover the dishes you cook? Check the number on the listing.
  3. Which accessories come in the box, and which would you have to buy separately?
  4. What does the warranty cover? We did not verify warranty terms; read the manufacturer’s statement on the listing.
  5. Does the appliance fit your counter and your storage space? See the cleanup and counter-space guide.

On claims

A printed claim is a claim, not a test result. We have not verified the functions, temperatures or accessory counts beyond what the listing titles state.
