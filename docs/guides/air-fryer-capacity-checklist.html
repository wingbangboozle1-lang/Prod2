A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

By the Editorial team (no named author supplied, FR-11)

Capacity figures are the first thing people compare, and they are easy to misread. This checklist keeps to what the listings state.

How the listings state capacity

  • Chefman 6-quart dual-basket air fryer: “6 Quart Dual Basket” in the title, with “2 Independent 3Qt” baskets. The title gives a total figure and a per-basket figure. Read both as stated.
  • Emeril Lagasse 26QT French door air fryer toaster oven: “26QT” in the title.
  • Chefman 8-quart TurboFry air fryer: “8 Qt” in the title.

Checklist

  1. Use one unit. Compare quarts with quarts, not with litres from another listing.
  2. Check whether a figure is per basket or total.
  3. Compare the figure each listing states. Do not assume two brands measure capacity the same way.
  4. Think about the food you cook most, and how many batches you would need.
  5. Check the dimensions on the listing (we did not extract them; FR-08) before you assume a larger number fits your counter.

What this guide does not do

It does not say a capacity is “enough” for any household, and it does not rank these products.
