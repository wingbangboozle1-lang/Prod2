The work behind it

Suggestion queue: choose the path before we build

Every proposed improvement, with its evidence, expected impact, effort and risk. Nothing in this queue is implemented until a reviewer accepts a path — this page is the review surface the brief asked for.

How to use this queue

Each card ends with our recommendation and the dependencies that gate it. To proceed: pick accept, amend, or reject per item (a comment in the repo's COORDINATION.md, or an instruction in the brief, is enough). Accepted items move to the decision log with their date, then to the build. Proposed order if you want our sequence:

  1. SG-06 — deploy measurement + Search Console. Nothing else compounds without it.
  2. SG-01 — brand rename after clearance (blocked on Q-19).
  3. SG-02 and SG-09 — cheap, on-thesis, low risk.
  4. SG-07 — the biggest content upgrade available, if real usage exists.

SG-01 Adopt a consumer-facing brand name (after trademark clearance)

proposed
Problem
The wordmark 'Prod2' is the project's established name, not a shop name. A real brand name would help the site read as a publication and support a future domain.
Proposal
Shortlist 2-3 candidate names (e.g. 'Counter & Crisp', 'The Basket Guide', 'Crisp Ledger'), run each through a trademark search (Q-19), pick one, rename in one pass (masthead, titles, OG card), then consider a domain.
Evidence
D-18 kept the established name only because clearance (Q-19) was open; the constraint is clearance, not the name itself.
Expected impact
Medium — brand recall, SERP click-through, linkability.
Effort
One session once a name clears.
Risk
Trademark collision if skipped; mitigated by the clearance step.
Depends on
Q-19

Our recommendation: Do it after clearance. Ranked 1 by impact/effort among brand items.

SG-05 Adopt a dated-snapshot price policy

proposed
Problem
Buyers want prices; the site withholds them (F-02) because they drift faster than re-verification.
Proposal
Publish price only with a mandatory read date + 'check listing' link; checks enforce price/date pairing; re-verify at each publishing pass. Ratings stay withheld (they invite selection bias).
Evidence
F-02; D-19 shows the dated-snapshot pattern already works for popularity claims.
Expected impact
Medium-high conversion relevance; adds upkeep to every pass.
Effort
Medium (rule + checks + one re-verification sweep).
Risk
Medium — stale prices damage trust more than absent prices; must be automated before adoption.
Depends on
Q-27

Our recommendation: Only after the automated date-pairing check exists. Do not do this by hand.

SG-07 Add a 'what it's like to live with' layer once real usage exists

proposed
Problem
S-06 (hands-on content) was recorded as not adopted because nobody has tested these appliances; listings can't tell a reader what cleanup or noise is like.
Proposal
Either (a) owner supplies first-hand notes/photos for one product and the site adds a clearly-labeled experience section, or (b) keep spec-only and say so. Do not fabricate experience.
Evidence
Google's thin-affiliation policy (S-GSPAM) rewards genuine experience; the current site is honest but thinner than ideal.
Expected impact
High on E-E-A-T, if real.
Effort
Depends entirely on real usage.
Risk
High if faked — never do this synthetically.
Depends on
Owner input

Our recommendation: Option (a) if the owner can supply real notes; otherwise stay honest with (b). This is the single biggest content upgrade available.

SG-12 Give every product its own buyer-first section: who it suits, what it does not do, what the listing says

Proposal
On each product block, lead with a 'Who it suits' line and a 'Where it falls short' line, both drawn only from listed facts (for example, 'Suits two dishes at the same temperature; the baskets are 3 qt each'). The sponsored link and paid-link label stay directly under that text.
Evidence
The existing product register (data/p2/products.json highlights); FTC guidance that endorsements must reflect honest experience (S-FTC2), which rules out invented hands-on claims.
Expected impact
Medium; likely the highest conversion-relevant change that needs no new data.
Effort
Small (copy in content/pages + one check that each block has both lines).
Risk
Low. Must not claim testing that did not happen (D-06).

—

Our recommendation: Accept. It improves the shop feel without adding claims.
