The work behind it

Suggestion queue: choose the path before we build

Every proposed improvement, with its evidence, expected impact, effort and risk. This page is the review surface the brief asked for: a place to compare paths and pick one before anything changes.

How to use this queue

Each card ends with our recommendation and the dependencies that gate it. To proceed: pick accept, amend, or reject per item (a comment in the repo's COORDINATION.md, or an instruction in the brief, is enough). Accepted items move to the decision log with their date, then to the build. Items the standing brief has already mandated show accepted or shipped with a progress note instead of waiting for a second nod.

The shortest credible path — the smallest sequence of owner decisions that unblocks the most work (SG-12 makes this standing):

  1. SG-06 — host the endpoint + verify Search Console (Q-P05). Nothing measures until this lands, and nothing compounds without measurement.
  2. SG-11 + SG-01 — pick the visual direction and the brand name in one decision, so the visual pass happens once (Q-P06).
  3. SG-13 — the token layer, immediately after: it makes the pick reversible.
  4. SG-14 — switch topic selection to measured queries after 14 days of data.
  5. SG-16 + SG-04 — open the question-capture loop once the consent basis exists (Q-47).

Design suggestions (the feed into the design prompt)

Written to be pasted into DESIGN_PROMPT.md whichever way the decision goes. All three keep the non-negotiable compliance block: disclosure above the fold, no prices or ratings, tagged sponsored links only, drawn-not-copied imagery.

SG-01 Adopt a consumer-facing brand name (after trademark clearance)

proposed
Problem
The wordmark 'Prod2' is the project's established name, not a shop name. A real brand name would help the site read as a publication and support a future domain.
Proposal
Shortlist 2-3 candidate names (e.g. 'Counter & Crisp', 'The Basket Guide', 'Crisp Ledger'), run each through a trademark search (Q-19), pick one, rename in one pass (masthead, titles, OG card), then consider a domain.
Evidence
D-18 kept the established name only because clearance (Q-19) was open; the constraint is clearance, not the name itself.
Expected impact
Medium — brand recall, SERP click-through, linkability.
Effort
One session once a name clears.
Risk
Trademark collision if skipped; mitigated by the clearance step.
Depends on
Q-19

Our recommendation: Do it after clearance. Ranked 1 by impact/effort among brand items.

SG-05 Adopt a dated-snapshot price policy

proposed
Problem
Buyers want prices; the site withholds them (F-02) because they drift faster than re-verification.
Proposal
Publish price only with a mandatory read date + 'check listing' link; checks enforce price/date pairing; re-verify at each publishing pass. Ratings stay withheld (they invite selection bias).
Evidence
F-02; D-19 shows the dated-snapshot pattern already works for popularity claims.
Expected impact
Medium-high conversion relevance; adds upkeep to every pass.
Effort
Medium (rule + checks + one re-verification sweep).
Risk
Medium — stale prices damage trust more than absent prices; must be automated before adoption.
Depends on
Q-27

Our recommendation: Only after the automated date-pairing check exists. Do not do this by hand.

SG-11 Design feed: three fully specified directions for the next visual pass

proposed
Problem
The brief asks for design suggestions that feed the design prompt, so a reviewer can pick the best path before anything is implemented. One direction (warm editorial) is carried and built; the alternatives have never been specified side by side.
Proposal
Publish three complete directions against the same compliance block: (A) Warm editorial - kitchen magazine that shows its sources: serif display (Fraunces), paper background, terracotta accent, drawn line-art only; this build follows it (DESIGN_PROMPT.md). (B) Clean modern shop - sans display (Inter), white cards, one strong accent color, product-first grid with big tap targets, tighter type scale; the most e-commerce-native of the three. (C) Bold magazine - high-contrast black/paper, oversized display numerals for specs, full-width section bands, editorial photography if the photography decision (Q-05/D-22 lineage, SG-18) ever clears. Each keeps: disclosure above the fold, no prices/ratings, sponsored-only product links, drawn-not-copied imagery.
Evidence
Owner brief 2026-11-19 (the site must look like a polished marketing affiliate publication, not generated output); carried D-18/DIR-A; DESIGN_PROMPT.md.
Expected impact
Decides the entire reader-facing look; direction B is the most shop-like, A is the most distinctive and least template-like, C is the boldest but needs photography.
Effort
The pick is one meeting; the token layer (SG-13) makes the implementation one session per direction.
Risk
Low if done through the token layer; high if done as scattered one-off CSS. Direction C depends on the photography decision.
Depends on
Q-P06

Our recommendation: Keep Direction A (already live, distinctive, cheapest to finish) unless the owner wants a more conventional shop look, in which case B. Decide at the same time as the brand name (SG-01) so the visual pass happens once.
