The work behind it

Privacy

Short version: this site collects almost nothing, on purpose.

What the site does

  • No cookies, no accounts, no sign-in. The site is static pages.
  • No third-party trackers. No ad networks, no analytics companies, no font CDNs, no social widgets.
  • Outbound click counting (the local counter) stores running counts in your own browser (localStorage). An outbound click is recorded as an ASIN and a target host only — never the full URL.
  • Nothing is transmitted to any server while the site's self-hosted analytics endpoint is unconfigured — which is its current state (F-19).

What the future endpoint would do (when deployed)

The self-hosted endpoint (analytics-endpoint/server.py) accepts five event names with a site path and optional ASIN, and stores daily counts per event and path. It never reads or stores IP addresses, user agents, referrers or cookies, and writes no request log. Its counts are aggregate numbers, not visitor records.

What leaves your browser when you click a product link

You go to Amazon. Amazon's own privacy terms apply there (Amazon Privacy Notice). Those links are paid links, as disclosed on every page.

The feed

The RSS feed is the no-data reader channel of this site: follow it and nothing about you is collected at all.

The question-capture form

The "What should we answer next?" form (front page and guides hub) is currently disconnected on purpose: it sends nothing to any server, and a typed question stays in your own browser. If the owner later connects a capture endpoint, the form will send only the question text, the optional email you entered, and your consent — and only when the consent box is checked. The consent basis is still being settled (Q-47), which is exactly why the form ships dark (F-P14).
