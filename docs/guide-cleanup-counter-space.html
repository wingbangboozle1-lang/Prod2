How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

Two promises listings make easily ("dishwasher safe", "compact") and two things buyers discover late. Here's how to read both before the box arrives.

2 min read · Every figure dated and sourced · How we check

Every countertop appliance signs a lease on your kitchen: a rectangle of counter, permanently, in exchange for dinners. The listings give you the square footage up front — most people just never read that part of the table.

1. Measure the footprint, not the vibe

The listed dimensions on this site's three air fryers:

  • P3 — 12.75″D × 13.5″W × 10″H
  • P1 — 13.38″D × 11.81″W × 10.63″H
  • P2 — 16.9″D × 15.8″W × 14.8″H

Depth is what eats a galley kitchen. Width is what blocks the drawer you open every day. And height matters more than people expect: a 10.63″ machine fits under standard upper cabinets with room to lift a basket; at 14.8″, P2 likely wants a clear wall. The capacity-per-square-inch analysis shows what each format returns for the space — as a checkable formula.

2. Count the parts, then picture Tuesday

Cleanup is a parts count:

  • P1 — two nonstick baskets, listed dishwasher-safe. Two zones to empty, one door's worth of convenience.
  • P3 — one XL nonstick basket, listed dishwasher-safe, plus a crisper tray in most machines of this type. One zone, fastest routine.
  • P2 — racks, a tray, and six included cooking accessories as listed. More capability per part; more parts per dinner.

"Dishwasher safe" is a listed claim about materials, not a guarantee about your machine's third rack — but it's the honest, checkable version of "easy to clean," which is not.

3. The trade-off, named

Capacity per square inch favors the oven at a cost: it's the deepest, the tallest, and the most to wash. The single basket is the minimalists' trade: less capacity shape flexibility, least counter. The dual basket is the compromise with a purpose — two dishes, two drawers, middle footprint.

There's no free option. There's only the trade you prefer. (That's the honest bottom line this site will give you — the numbers to weigh it are in the table.)

One measurement to take today

Open the cabinet above your counter and measure the clear height. Then measure the depth of the counter space you'd actually give up. That pair of numbers eliminates more options than any review — and it's why this guide exists.
