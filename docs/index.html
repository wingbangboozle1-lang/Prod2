Questions readers actually ask

Short answers, each one pointing at the page where you can check it.

Which air fryer should I buy?

It depends on two numbers you already have: how deep your counter is and whether you cook two dishes at once. Four questions on the decision page will name one machine and quote the listed fact the answer rests on. The short version: under 13 inches of depth, the single-basket machine is the only one on this shelf that fits; two dishes that must finish together point at the dual basket; rack and sheet-pan cooking points at the French-door oven.

Why are there no prices on this site?

Because a price we cannot fetch from Amazon is a price we would be making up. Amazon's Program Policies restrict how prices may be displayed, and a price cached from last week is worse than no price at all. Every buy button goes to the live listing, where the current price is Amazon's own and always current (F-02).

Why no star ratings or "best" badge?

Two reasons, both recorded. Star ratings are Amazon's content and their display is governed by the Associates agreement, so this site does not republish them. And nobody here has cooked with these machines, so a "best" badge would be an opinion dressed as a test result — the publish gate fails the build if the site claims hands-on testing (about the site).

How do you pick which products to follow?

The owner selected five: three air-fryer formats and two thermometer workflows. This site adds no others and ranks none of them. What it does is read each official listing, date the read, and publish the values with the link they came from (the source register).

What if a listing changes after you read it?

Then our figure is stale and says so: every value carries the date it was read, and each product card names the most recent re-check. Where a listing states a value two ways, both readings are kept rather than averaged (the flagged page lists every such case).

Do the links cost me anything?

No. They are Amazon Associates links: the price you pay is the listing's price, and a qualifying purchase may earn this site a commission. That is the disclosure at the top of every page, and it is repeated in the footer (how disclosures are handled).
