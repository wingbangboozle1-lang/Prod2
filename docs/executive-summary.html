The work behind it

Executive summary

What this project is, what is verified, what is missing, and what decisions are open — on one page. Written for a reviewer deciding what to do next.

What this is

A small affiliate publication (Prod2 — Kitchen notes & buying guides) built around five owner-supplied Amazon products: three air fryers (dual-basket, oven-style, single-basket) and two meat thermometers (instant-read, wireless leave-in). The reader-facing site is a question-led buying guide; the machinery behind it — verification register, intent records, flags, open questions, suggestion queue, decision log, measurement plan — stays reachable from one navigation group and every footer.

The five products and their status

  • P1–P3 (ASINs B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH) — verified from official listings since 2026-11-14, re-checked on dated passes through 2026-11-19. Specification tables carried from those dated reads; titles and breadcrumbs re-read 2026-11-19.
  • P4–P5 (ASINs B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z) — the brief's two additional short links, resolved and read for the first time on 2026-11-19. They are meat thermometers, not air fryers; the site organizes by format accordingly (decision D-P01).
  • Links: all five owner-submitted short links resolve to the ASINs above with no tag= parameter (flag F-03). The site publishes tagged Special Links (https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20, decision D-01) with rel="sponsored" and the required disclosure on every page. Whether the tracking ID is registered and whether clicks attribute is confirmable only inside Associates Central (F-57, F-58) — the site claims no revenue.

What is deliberately absent

  • Prices and star ratings — withheld (F-02): they drift faster than honest re-verification.
  • Commission figures per product — blank (F-01): the official rate table's category bands don't map to these ASINs; no rate is inferred.
  • Hands-on testing — none claimed (D-06): this is a specification publication until real usage exists (queued as SG-07).
  • Forecasts — no conversion score or revenue forecast anywhere; the campaign ledger starts empty by rule.

Measurement and automation

The build is automated end to end: tools/build.py renders the site from the data registers; tools/checks.py is the publish gate (disclosure placement, sponsored-link marking, unique titles/meta, sourced facts, link integrity) and runs from a clean checkout with standard-library Python. Measurement ships as machinery but not as data: the privacy-preserving click counter and the self-hosted endpoint are deployable (contract here), and nothing is transmitted until an endpoint is configured (F-19). The five defined ledger events and their official attribution windows are recorded on the measurement page.

Open decisions for the reviewer

  1. Run the one-link-per-product test in Associates Central — gates attribution claims and scaling (Q-01, F-58).
  2. Host the analytics endpoint + enable Search Console — top operational priority (SG-06).
  3. Brand rename after trademark clearance — SG-01, blocked on Q-19.
  4. Whether to publish prices under a dated-snapshot rule — SG-05, only with automation.
