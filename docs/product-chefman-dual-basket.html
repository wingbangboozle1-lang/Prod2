Who it suits

Two dishes, one finish time

  • Weeknight two-component dinners. Protein in one zone, vegetables in the other, each with its own settings; the listed Sync Finish exists to land them together.
  • Small households that cook in halves. Each zone holds 3 qt listed — under half of the 8 qt single basket, and well under a tenth of the 26 qt oven cavity. The capacity checklist makes quarts comparable before you decide.
  • People who rinse, not scrub. The title lists the baskets as nonstick and dishwasher-safe (read 2026-11-19).
  • Counters with 13.4″ of depth. That is the listed depth; see the measurement note below.

Who it doesn't suit

Whole roasts and shallow counters

  • One big item, cooked whole. Two half-size zones cannot take what a single 8 qt basket (P3) or the 26 qt oven (P2) can. Nothing large goes in whole here.
  • A counter shallower than ~13.4″. The listed 13.38″D is the second-deepest of the three fryers; the shallowest is P3 at 12.75″.
  • Anyone shopping by price alone. We do not publish prices — they move hourly and the only current source is the listing (F-02).

Measure first: the counter math

From the listed dimensions — 13.38″D × 11.81″W × 10.63″H — the footprint is about 158 in² (depth × width), and the listed 6 qt against 13.38″ of depth is 0.45 qt per inch of depth. Those two are derived numbers: the formula is the point, and the counter-space assessment shows the same math for all three air fryers. Add door swing and vent clearance to whatever your cabinet allows; the listing's numbers are the box, not the installation.
