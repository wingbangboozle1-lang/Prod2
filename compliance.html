The work behind it

Compliance gate: disclosures and link policy

How the Associates disclosure, the sponsored-link marking and the endorsement rules are enforced in the publishing workflow — automatically where possible, by gate where not.

Disclosure placement (enforced by the build gate)

  • Under the masthead on every page, above the content — including the front page and this one — carrying the required sentence verbatim: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
  • Repeated in every footer, with a link to this page.
  • Product links carry the (paid link) label next to the call to action.
  • tools/checks.py fails the build if any page lacks the disclosure strip or the footer sentence.

Outbound link policy (enforced by the build gate)

  • Every product link is an Amazon Associates Special Link of the form https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20 — the final destination URL with the tag as a parameter, no shortener (Participation Requirements 2(b), 6(v)–(w): a link must not obscure where it goes).
  • Every product link carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" per Google's guidance on paid links (S-GOUT) — without replacing the required disclosure.
  • The owner-supplied short links are recorded in the intent record verbatim and are not judged valid or invalid (FLG-003 unresolved).
  • tools/checks.py fails the build on any amazon.com/dp/ link missing the tag or the sponsored rel.

Endorsement and review context

  • This site publishes listed specifications, not product endorsements in the FTC's testimonial sense: nobody here has used the products (D-06), and the site says so.
  • Manufacturer claims ("±0.5°F accuracy", "NIST-certified", "1000°F heat safe") are recorded as the listing's statements with dates — never adopted as this site's own claims.
  • Popularity statements are allowed only as dated snapshots of an official page (D-19); "trending", "best-selling" and similar bare adjectives are banned by the checks.

The rules we check against (official sources)

  • Associates Program Operating Agreement (S-OA) — including Section 5 on identification.
  • Associates Program Policies (S-POL) — Participation Requirements 2(b), 6(v), 6(w).
  • Associates help: identifying yourself as an Associate (S-HELP).
  • FTC Disclosures 101 (S-FTC1) and FTC Endorsement Guides (S-FTC2).
  • Google spam policies (thin affiliation) (S-GSPAM) — why this site leads with questions and sourced tables.

What is not yet verified (and is claimed nowhere)

The tracking ID's registration and activity (F-57) and the one-link-per-product reporting test (F-58) are owner-side actions inside Associates Central. Until they pass, the site publishes the links and the disclosure but claims no attributed click, order or revenue.
